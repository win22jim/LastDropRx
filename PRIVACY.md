# Privacy Policy

**Last Drop Rx**
Effective date: March 28, 2026

## Overview

Last Drop Rx does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. Your privacy is fundamental to how this app is built.

## Data Storage

All medication data — including medications, vials, doses, and settings — is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud account. Only you can access this data.

## Apple Health

Last Drop Rx can optionally read medication and dose data from Apple Health with your explicit permission. This data is used solely within the app on your device to sync dose entries. It is never sent to any external server.

## Third Parties

Last Drop Rx uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

## iCloud Sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, your app data syncs across your devices through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to the developer.
